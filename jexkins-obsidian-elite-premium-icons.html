04 — FREQUENTLY ASKED

Before you deploy.

Quick answers to help you get started with Jexkins.

How do I add Jexkins?

Use any invite button on this page and complete Discord's authorisation flow. You must have the appropriate permissions in the target server.

Does this page show live bot statistics?

No. The console is a visual interface preview, not a live telemetry feed. Real uptime, latency and server counts should only be shown once connected to a reliable data source.

Where do I get support?

Join the Jexkins support server using the Support Hub link or contact the developer by email.

Where are the legal policies?

Use the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links in the footer. Ensure the pages are published and accurately describe the bot's actual data practices.
